NIVARA — AI-Powered Municipal Waterlogging Decision-Support Platform

Secured 1st Runner-Up with Team PanchaBhuj for NIVARA, an AI-powered municipal waterlogging decision-support platform designed for Kolkata. NIVARA is an integrated software platform that maps city drainage positions and tackles urban waterlogging through four core operational capabilities:

1

Predictive Waterlogging Intelligence

Automatically predicts waterlogging probabilities and risk levels across 166+ localities by integrating live weather forecasts (via Open-Meteo API) with a trained Random Forest machine learning model factoring in terrain elevation, slope, and soil moisture.

2

Real-Time Citizen Reporting

Enables local residents to submit real-time reports of drain blockages and standing water, complete with original photographs, water depth, and GPS coordinates.

3

Automated Administrative Ward Mapping

Uses spatial Point-in-Polygon (PIP) joins to automatically assign each report to its exact Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC) ward or Urban Local Body (ULB).

4

Municipal Command Dashboard

Displays an interactive map with color-coded risk indicators (High, Medium, Low) and citizen-reported issues so municipal engineers can prioritize desilting and dispatch response teams efficiently.
